# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Hex?

Hex is a community-driven platform for creating, sharing, and discovering Minecraft capes.

## Is a Minecraft account required?

Yes. A valid Minecraft online or offline account is required to use Hex.

## Is Hex free to use?

Yes.

Hex is free to use and core functionality will remain available to everyone.

## Does Hex support Minecraft Bedrock Edition?

Yes. Hex has a Bedrock Pack Generator at [https://dash.hexcapes.qzz.io/bedrock](https://dash.hexcapes.qzz.io/bedrocks) that lets you browse the full cape library and generate `.mcpack` files for Bedrock Edition. You can search and filter capes, preview them on a 3D model, and download a ready-to-install pack.

## Who can see my capes?

Anyone using the Hex mod can view public capes through the platform.

## Does Hex store my Minecraft credentials?

No.

Hex never stores your Minecraft password or account credentials.

Authentication is handled through supported account verification methods.

## Is Hex affiliated with Mojang Studios?

No.

Hex is an independent community project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mojang Studios.

## Is Hex affiliated with Microsoft or Xbox?

No.

Hex is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft Corporation or Xbox.

## Are uploaded capes reviewed?

Hex may review, remove, or restrict content that violates platform rules or community guidelines.

## Can my cape be removed?

Yes.

Capes that violate platform rules, impersonate others, contain inappropriate content, or abuse platform systems may be removed.

## Who owns the capes I upload?

You retain ownership of the content you create and upload.

By uploading content to Hex, you grant Hex permission to store, process, and display the content as required to operate the platform.

## Is Hex open source?

Check the project's official repositories and announcements for current open-source availability and licensing information.

## Where can I get support?

Support is available through the Hex Discord server.
